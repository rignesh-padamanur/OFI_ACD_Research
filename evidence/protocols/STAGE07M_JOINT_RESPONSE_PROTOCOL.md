# Stage 07m: joint initial, subsequent and total price movement

Fixed before this stage's empirical summaries, 2 October 2026. This implements the common-row J/C/I interpretation in the narrowed research scope. It is descriptive accounting, not a new regression, detector, causal design or permanence classification. Ignore manuscript.tex. No data purchase or live acquisition.

## Research question and unchanged parents

How much recorded price displacement is already present at first detection, and where is price relative to that starting displacement at 60 and 300 seconds? J is detection-second displacement, C subsequent displacement, and I total displacement, all in pips aligned to the inherited onset quote-imbalance sign. Consequently J can be positive, zero or negative. The primary horizon remains 300 seconds; 60 seconds is secondary.

## Common observations and endpoint descriptions

Preserve every main-C row in a membership ledger. Joint descriptions use only rows also eligible for J and I; save their losses relative to each unchanged main-C sample. Reconcile I=J+C with inherited absolute tolerance 1e-9 pips and relative tolerance 1e-12. Compare exact stored signs and zeros, never round, divide by J, floor a denominator or apply a new materiality threshold. Validate J invariance across horizons and consistency with direct marks.

For nonzero J, use five disjoint endpoint descriptions: unchanged since detection (C=0); farther in initial direction (C and J have the same sign); partial retracement (C opposite J, I same sign as J); at pre-onset mark (I=0, C opposite J); beyond pre-onset mark (I opposite J, C opposite J). Require a consistent sign combination and exactly one description. For J=0, keep positive, negative and zero final displacement as three separate descriptions. Exact ties use the directly stored outcomes, not I recomputed through J+C. Very small nonzero J remains mathematically nonzero, without a claim of economically important displacement.

Endpoint ordering is strict: extension requires abs(I)>abs(J), and partial retracement requires 0<abs(I)<abs(J). Unchanged requires exact I=J; zero J requires exact C=I, as the shared direct price marks imply. An inconsistent sign/order combination stops rather than being repaired through the additive-identity tolerance.

These describe endpoints, not monotonic paths, first-crossing times, permanence or economic regimes. J-sign groups condition on a realized detection-time price outcome, not a strictly pre-onset state. Negative C alone is not evidence of reversal: J=-1,C=-0.5 gives I=-1.5 and extends the initial movement. J=1,C=-0.2 is partial retracement. J=0,C=1 has no initial move to retain.
